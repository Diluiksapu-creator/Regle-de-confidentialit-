This policy explains what information the mobile game Planet Breaker: Idle Destroyer (the "Game", package com.destroythatplanet.game) collects, why, and who it is shared with. The Game is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • You do not need to create an account to play. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device, and in your Google Play Games cloud save if you are signed in to Google Play Games.
  • The Game uses third-party services from Google and Unity for analytics, crash reports, optional rewarded ads, in-app purchases and cloud saves. These services may collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

The Game may also send local notifications (for example when your cargo is full). They are generated on your device and can be turned off in your phone settings.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

4. Advertising choices

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings, then Google, then Ads). Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

5. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention and deletion

  • Progress stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game, or with the "Reset progress" button in the Game settings.
  • Cloud saves can be deleted from your Google Play Games settings.
  • Analytics data is kept by Google Firebase for a limited period (by default 14 months) and then deleted.
  • To ask for deletion of any other data linked to you, email us at the address below. We will answer within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or limit its processing. To exercise these rights, contact us. You can also file a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Data sent by the Game and its service providers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use the security measures provided by these services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
